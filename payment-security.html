Payment, Security & Currency

Last updated:

Cards We Accept

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover.

Transaction Currency

All prices are listed and all transactions are processed in U.S. dollars (USD).

How We Protect Your Payment Details

Payment card information is entered on secure, encrypted pages and transmitted over TLS-encrypted connections. Card transactions are processed by our PCI-DSS-compliant payment provider. Gridiron Beef does not receive or store your full payment card number on its own systems. We maintain reasonable safeguards designed to protect the information involved in your purchase.

Billing

Your card is charged at the time your order is placed, except for bulk cattle shares and similar pre-orders, where a deposit is charged at checkout and the remaining balance is charged as described on the applicable product before the order ships. For any subscription or recurring orders, you will receive an email notice before each recurring charge, and you may cancel before the billing date without penalty.
