Privacy Policy

Last updated:

American Quality Protein LLC, doing business as Gridiron Beef ("Gridiron Beef," "we," "us," or "our"), respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit gridironbeef.com or place an order, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

  • Order and contact information you provide at checkout: name, billing and shipping address, email address, and telephone number.
  • Payment information: your card details are entered into and processed by our PCI-DSS-compliant payment processor. We do not receive or store full payment card numbers on our own systems.
  • Communications: messages you send us by email, phone, or through the chat feature on our website.
  • Automatically collected data: standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, and pages viewed, collected through cookies and similar technologies to operate and improve the site.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process and fulfill orders, arrange shipping of perishable products, communicate with you about your order, provide customer service, prevent fraud, comply with legal obligations, and, where permitted, send you promotional messages you may opt out of at any time.

How We Share Your Information

We share information only as needed to run the business: with our payment processor to complete transactions; with shipping and fulfillment providers to deliver your order; with service providers that help us operate the website and its features (including our order/checkout platform and the third-party service that powers our website chat feature, which receives messages you submit through it); and with authorities where required by law. We do not sell your personal information.

Data Security

Payment card data is transmitted over encrypted (TLS) connections and handled by our PCI-DSS-compliant processor. We maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices and Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may opt out of marketing communications, by contacting us using the details below. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable state privacy laws. We will respond consistent with those laws.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the site, remember your cart, and understand site usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect how the site functions.

Children

Our site and products are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
