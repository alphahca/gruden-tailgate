Freezer Size Guide

1/8 Steer (~40 lbs Boxed)

Easily fits in a standard home refrigerator-freezer combo compartment. Requires roughly 1.5 to 2.0 cubic feet of space.

1/4 Steer (~80 lbs Boxed)

Requires a small standalone chest or upright freezer. Will take up approximately 3.5 to 5.0 cubic feet of space.

1/2 Steer (~160 lbs Boxed)

Requires a dedicated medium-sized chest freezer. Expect to use roughly 7.0 to 10.0 cubic feet of space.

Whole Steer (~320 lbs Boxed)

Requires a large, heavy-duty chest freezer. You will need a minimum of 14.0 to 21.0 cubic feet to comfortably store the entire yield.
