Freezer Size Guide

1/8 Cattle (~64 lbs Boxed)

Fits in a standard home refrigerator-freezer with room to spare, or a compact standalone unit. Requires roughly 2.5 to 3.5 cubic feet of space.

1/4 Cattle (~129 lbs Boxed)

Requires a small-to-medium standalone chest or upright freezer. Will take up approximately 5.5 to 8.0 cubic feet of space.

1/2 Cattle (~262.5 lbs Boxed)

Requires a dedicated medium-sized chest freezer. Expect to use roughly 7.0 to 10.0 cubic feet of space.

Whole Cattle (~525 lbs Boxed)

Requires a large, heavy-duty chest freezer. You will need a minimum of 14.0 to 21.0 cubic feet to comfortably store the entire yield.
